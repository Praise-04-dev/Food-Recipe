🛒 INGREDIENTS
  • 340g spaghetti
  • 450g ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried italian seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (for serving)
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

Spaghetti

with Meat Sauce

♥︎

Classic Italian dish made with long, thin pasta and a rich meat sauce.

INSTRUCTIONS
  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat.
    Add onionand cook until soft, about3-4 mintues.
  3. Add garlic and cook for30 second until fragrant.
  4. Add ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon
    Drain excess fat if needed.
  5. Stir in crushed tomatoes,tomato paste,italian seasoning,salt,pepper, and red pepper flakes(if using).
  6. Bring to a simmer and cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Toss the cooked spagetti with the meat sauce,
    Serve hot with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley,Enjoy!

Notes

  • You can use any type of pasta you like, such as penne or fettuccine.
  • For a vegetarian version, substitute the ground beef with lentils or a plant-based meat alternative.
  • Feel free to add vegetables like bell peppers, mushrooms, or zucchini to the sauce for extra flavor and nutrition.
  • Leftover sauce can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days or frozen for up to 3 months.
